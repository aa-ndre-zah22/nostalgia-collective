🎨 Visualize Your Memory

Once your soundscape is ready, sketch what you see, feel, and remember. Our intuitive drawing canvas makes it easy to express yourself without needing any artistic skills.

  • Free-form drawing tools
  • Warm color palette (16+ colors)
  • Multiple brush styles
  • Undo/Redo functionality
  • Sound plays while drawing
  • Auto-save every 30 seconds
  • Export as image

🎥 Draw Together, Live

Host a live drawing room where up to 4 people can draw on the same canvas while video chatting. Perfect for creating memories with friends who share your childhood era.

  • Live video calls (WebRTC)
  • Synchronized shared canvas
  • See cursor positions in real-time
  • Live chat integration
  • Synchronized sound playback
  • Record and export sessions
  • Invite via shareable links
